#ethics-security
In general, at the moment, there are three main goals that companies care about, or rather must at least be seen to care about:
- Environmental Impact
- Economic Feasibility
- Social Equity
##### Ethical Dilemmas
- Performance VS Sustainability (the product performs well, but it uses tons of power)
- Cost VS Accessibility (the product is great, but too expensive to be truly mass-market)
- Short-term VS Long-term
##### Sustainable system design
- Optimise algorithms to reduce processing and energy usage
- Physical designs should be recyclable, upgradable or otherwise reduce e-waste
- Compress data to reduce carbon footprint from data storage
