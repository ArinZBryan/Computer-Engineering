#hardware/electronics/circuits/components
Unlike [passive circuit components](Passive%20Circuit%20Components.md), which do not generate energy, only absorb, store or dissipate it, active components can deliver energy or control or amplify signals.
### Voltage Sources
A two-terminal device that maintains a potential difference between the two terminals. The idealised voltage source will always maintain the specified voltage, regardless of the current that would need to flow through it to maintain that. The current is determined by [Ohm's law](Circuit%20Laws.md#Ohm's%20Law). An idealised voltage source also has no internal resistance. This may optionally be shown as a resistor in series with the voltage source, sometimes in a box with the voltage source. 
##### Example
Batteries, External Power Supplies
### Current Sources
A two-terminal device that maintains a current flowing through it. An idealised current source will always maintain the specified current, regardless of the potential difference that would need to be across it to maintain that. The voltage is determined by [Ohm's law](Circuit%20Laws.md#Ohm's%20Law).
##### Example
The closest component to being a current source is a photovoltaic cell (solar panel), but even this is not _really_ a current source.
### Diodes
A diode is a two-terminal device that allows current to flow primarily in one direction. Unlike most passive components, it also has a non-linear I-V curve.
