#systematic-design 
### Systematic Thinking
Systematic thinking is a lens through which the behaviour of complex systems are analysed that originates from a field called systems theory. It emphasises the interactions of components of a system have with other components across the whole system. and their resulting feedback-loops, interdependencies and emergent behaviours.
### Systemic Design
Systemic design is an approach for the design of systems that attempts to address the challenges brought up by systematic thinking.
### Systematic Design
_Systematic_ design (not systemic design, see above) is an approach to solving design problems that focuses on the method by which they are solved. It emphasises repeatability, traceability and logical decomposition of the problem down into manageable parts that can be solved more easily.
